Dr. Xiaofan Li

I am an Assistant Professor at Nanjing University (QS 90), China, and a DAAD AInet Fellow, Germany. I previously worked as a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, and as a researcher at Leibniz University Hannover and the L3S Research Center, Germany. I received my Ph.D. from Swinburne University of Technology, Australia, and my bachelor's degree from Wuhan University, China. My research focuses on databases and graph databases, with a current interest in the intersection of database systems and large language models (LLMs). My work has appeared in leading database conferences, including SIGMOD and ICDE, and other major venues.

I am currently looking for motivated PhD, master's, and undergraduate students who are passionate about research. If you are interested in working with me, please feel free to get in touch.
